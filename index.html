A few things to know about the energy behind your everyday appliances.

A kilowatt-hour (kWh) is a unit of energy. Using a 1,000 W appliance for one hour consumes 1 kWh; using a 100 W appliance for ten hours consumes the same amount.

Look for the usage charge, expressed in cents per kWh, on your Australian electricity bill. The 30 cents in this calculator is an example, not a current market average. For time-of-use plans, use the rate that applies when you run the appliance.

This calculator assumes constant power use. Appliances can draw different amounts as settings and operating cycles change. Daily supply charges, standby use, solar exports and other appliances are outside this estimate.

Keep hours of use and the electricity rate the same, then compare the energy or cost values. For televisions, compare models of similar screen size and check the product's actual energy rating before buying.
