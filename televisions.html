03

What should a shopper do?

A practical shortlist

Choose the size that fits. Compare the annual kWh on similar TVs.

Once you know the screen size and features you need, put two or three models side by side. A lower labelled kWh/year means a lower estimated energy cost under the same test conditions. Use your own electricity usage rate to estimate dollars. The star rating is most meaningful for TVs of similar size and features.

04

Read the evidence carefully

What we analysed

The supplied tv_2026_02_15.csv contains 4,724 registry rows. We kept 4,508 entries marked Available and listing Australia in SoldIn, with valid positive screen size and labelled kWh/year. The charts use medians and linearly interpolated percentiles. Full counts and reproducible steps are in the README.

What it cannot tell us

These are registered product entries, not sales or ownership. The annual label is a standardised estimate, based on 10 hours of viewing plus 14 hours of standby per day; real settings and usage change the result. Rows may represent multiple registrations or variants of a model. The data alone cannot rank popular TVs, guarantee availability in a shop or prove a brand is better.

Source context: Energy Rating registered product data and label methodology. The exact provenance and licence of the supplied local CSV still need to be confirmed before publication beyond class work.
