One appliance at a time.

Making invisible energy a little more visible.

Appliances are part of daily life, but the electricity they use can be hard to picture. Wattwise connects an appliance's power rating with how long you use it, so you can explore consumption and estimated cost together. Its Televisions page goes one step further and asks whether more energy-label stars really mean less electricity.

Created by Huy Tran for COS30045 Data Visualisation at Swinburne, this project explores appliance energy use in an Australian household context. The television story uses a 3 October 2026 registration snapshot, analysed in a KNIME workflow.

How the numbers work

  1. 01
    Start with power and time.

    Daily energy (kWh) = power (W) x daily use (hours) ÷ 1,000.

  2. 02
    Extend the same daily routine.

    Monthly estimates use 30 days. Annual estimates use 365 days.

  3. 03
    Apply your electricity usage rate.

    Cost (AUD) = energy (kWh) x rate (cents per kWh) ÷ 100.

An honest view of the data

Examples, with context

The appliance wattages, television models and default electricity rate are illustrative placeholders. They are not measured data, actual product specifications or an Australian market average. For your own estimates, use your appliance's rating and the usage rate on your bill.

A star rating shows efficiency relative to screen size, so it does not tell you total electricity use. Compare kWh/year when TV sizes differ, and use stars when sizes are similar. Calculator comparisons assume constant power use, and labelled TV energy assumes standard test conditions, so actual consumption can change with settings, operating cycles and standby behaviour. For official Australian energy rating information, including how TV star ratings work, visitettings, operating cycles and standby behaviour. For official Australian energy rating information, visit Energy Rating ↗.

The Televisions page is the Exercise 3 data story. It uses the supplied 3 October 2026 television registration snapshot and a 29-node KNIME workflow with two executed Bar Chart views and one Line Plot view. It compares star ratings with labelled annual energy use across screen sizes.
