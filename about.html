One appliance at a time.

Making invisible energy a little more visible.

Appliances are part of daily life, but the electricity they use can be hard to picture. Wattwise connects an appliance's power rating with how long you use it, so you can explore consumption and estimated cost together.

Created by Huy Tran for COS30049 Data Visualisation at Swinburne, this student project explores appliance energy consumption in an Australian household context.

How the numbers work

  1. 01
    Start with power and time.

    Daily energy (kWh) = power (W) × daily use (hours) ÷ 1,000.

  2. 02
    Extend the same daily routine.

    Monthly estimates use 30 days. Annual estimates use 365 days.

  3. 03
    Apply your electricity usage rate.

    Cost (AUD) = energy (kWh) × rate (cents per kWh) ÷ 100.

An honest view of the data

Examples, with context

The appliance wattages, television models and default electricity rate are illustrative placeholders. They are not measured data, actual product specifications or an Australian market average. For your own estimates, use your appliance's rating and the usage rate on your bill.

Comparisons assume constant power use. Actual consumption can change with appliance settings, operating cycles and standby behaviour. For official Australian energy rating information, visit Energy Rating ↗.

The Insights page is a separate analysis scaffold for Assignment 1. Its dataset has not been selected, its proposed KNIME workflow has not been executed, and no analytical findings are published yet.
